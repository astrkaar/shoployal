📐 Segmentation Rules

Tune per business type — a cafe and a clothing brand shouldn't use the same thresholds

How time-tracking works: every bill is saved with the exact timestamp it was made. "Days since last visit" is calculated live as (right now − timestamp of that customer's most recent bill) — it's never a stored/stale number, it updates the instant you load any page.

Example presets — Cafe: At-risk 14 days, VIP 5 visits / 60-day window. Clothing brand: At-risk 60 days, VIP 3 visits / 180-day window.

🤖 Automated Coupon Engine

This replaces manually deciding who gets a coupon. It scans live segments and issues coupons on its own.
